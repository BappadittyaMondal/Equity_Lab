## 3. Investor Entry Timeline & Detailed Investment Thesis

### 1. Concord Control Systems Ltd
* **When Picked:** Mukul Agrawal & family entered in early 2023 (~5.56% combined stake). Ashish Kacholia entered in late 2023 / early 2024 (~1.64% stake).
* **Thesis & Rationale:** 
  1. Indian Railways modernization & mandatory rollout of *Kavach* (Automatic Train Protection).
  2. Proprietary electronics, couplers, and lighting controls with near-zero competition in specialized RDSO-approved niches.
  3. Stellar financial metrics: ROCE of 31.2%, conservative debt (D/E 0.25), and net profit compounding from ₹13 Cr in FY24 to ₹42 Cr in FY26.

### 2. Advait Energy Transitions Ltd (formerly Advait Infratech)
* **When Picked:** Ashish Kacholia entered via preferential allotment/market purchases in 2023 (~1.83%). Vijay Kedia entered via Kedia Securities in 2023 (~1.14%).
* **Thesis & Rationale:**
  1. Green Energy & Hydrogen transition: Manufacturing of electrolyzers, fuel cells, and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).
  2. Power Transmission & Substation infrastructure beneficiary of India's massive transmission grid expansion.
  3. Robust 5-year profit growth CAGR of ~60%, low leverage (D/E 0.36), and healthy ROCE of 26.0%.

### 3. Vasa Denticity Ltd (Dentalkart)
* **When Picked:** Mukul Agrawal entered mid-2023 (~2.36%). Ashish Kacholia entered mid-2023 (~1.28%, trimmed from peak >3.5%).
* **Thesis & Rationale:**
  1. Pioneer B2B dental consumables & equipment marketplace (Dentalkart) serving 50,000+ clinics across 15,000+ pin codes.
  2. Network effects: Organizing a heavily fragmented, high-margin dental supply chain.
  3. Clean balance sheet: Zero debt (D/E 0.00), strong operating cash flows, and 35%+ quarterly top-line expansion.

### 4. Neuland Laboratories Ltd
* **When Picked:** Mukul Agrawal entered in 2020-2021 (~3.12%, his largest holding by value, ~₹800+ Cr). Vijay Kedia held briefly in 2020-2021 (~1.0%) before exiting.
* **Thesis & Rationale:**
  1. Transformation from generic API commoditization into high-margin CDMO (Custom Development & Manufacturing Organization) and complex peptide synthesis.
  2. Margin expansion: EBITDA margins expanded from ~12% to >28% due to commercialization of innovator APIs.
  3. Compounding machine: 26.5% ROCE, low leverage (D/E 0.11), and high regulatory compliance records with USFDA.

### 5. Goldiam International Ltd
* **When Picked:** Ramesh Damani entered 2018-2019 (~1.58%, trimmed sub-1%). Mukul Agrawal entered 2021 (~2.29%). Ashish Kacholia entered Dec 2022 (~1.01%, trimmed sub-1%).
* **Thesis & Rationale:**
  1. First-mover advantage in Lab-Grown Diamond (LGD) jewelry exports to US mega-retailers (Walmart, Signet, Macy's).
  2. Asset-light, zero-debt balance sheet (D/E 0.02) generating 25.8% ROCE with consistent 4-5% dividend yield.
  3. Consumer secular tailwind: Mass adoption of affordable, conflict-free LGD fine jewelry.

### 6. Vikran Engineering Ltd
* **When Picked:** Ashish Kacholia & Mukul Agrawal entered in pre-IPO rounds (2024-2025) and hold post-listing equity (listed Sep 2025).
* **Thesis & Rationale:**
  1. Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) player across high-voltage power transmission, water infrastructure (Jal Jeevan Mission), and rail electrification.
  2. Solid order book execution visibility with public infrastructure tailwinds.

### 7. Raghav Productivity Enhancers Ltd
* **When Picked:** Rekha Jhunjhunwala entered late 2021 (~5.2%). Ashish Kacholia entered Nov 2022 (~2.02%). Mukul Agrawal entered Dec 2022 (~1.55%). *(All have trimmed below 1% or exited as of 2025-2026)*.
* **Thesis & Rationale:**
  1. Global monopoly in quartz-based ramming mass for steel induction furnaces; high entry barriers via captive mines.
  2. 30.3% ROCE with zero debt.
  3. Superstars entered around ₹200-₹400; once the stock scaled 5x-8x to ₹1,800+ and P/E reached 133x, they took aggressive profits.

### 8. Repro India Ltd
* **When Picked:** Vijay Kedia entered 2016-2017 (~6.32%, sitting tight). Ashish Kacholia & Mukul Agrawal entered 2021-2022 and trimmed below 1%.
* **Thesis & Rationale:**
  1. Shift from capital-intensive offset printing to zero-inventory digital Print-on-Demand (POD) for publishers via Amazon/Flipkart.
  2. Slow order ramp-up and top-line contraction (-10% YoY) prompted Kacholia and Agrawal to cut exposure, while Kedia maintains a long-term turnaround stance.
