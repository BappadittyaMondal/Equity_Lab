## 🔍 PART D: WHY EACH RANK WAS ASSIGNED

### 🥇 #1 — PIXTRANS
The only stock in the merged 37-stock universe with: (a) 13 years audited data, (b) stable ROCE band 19–27% for all 5 years, (c) PAT growth +89% YoY in Q1 FY27, (d) CFO/PAT = 1.30x (real cash), (e) D/E = 0.03. No single metric is flashy but the combination is rare. Every competitor to an industrial belt manufacturer who has served European OEMs for 20 years must first replicate 20 years of JIT certifications. That's the moat.

### 🥈 #2 — DYCL (Dynamic Cables)
CFO/PAT = **2.48x** — the highest in the entire merged universe. This means for every ₹100 of reported profit, ₹248 arrives as actual cash. This happens in businesses where customers pay advances (grid utilities). The ROCE of 26% has been perfectly consistent for 4 years. The new Rajasthan solar cable plant targets the US solar installation market, where India's cost advantage is structural.

### 🥉 #3 — JSLL (Jeena Sikho Lifecare)
ROCE = **64%** (highest in core confirmed stocks). Business model: patients pre-pay 21–45 day treatment packages before receiving treatment = negative working capital. The stock is −43% from its 52-week high despite improving fundamentals — this is the most asymmetric entry setup in this list. D/E = 0.27 is the only flag.

### #4 — NITTAGELA
13 years of data. Pharmaceutical gelatin cannot be substituted without regulatory re-approval (US FDA, EU GMP) that takes 3–4 years. Japan (Nitta Inc) holds the global IP. Priced at 14.5x P/E with 28% ROCE and D/E = 0.01. This is the definition of a misvalued quality compounder.

### #5 — INDOTECH
The ROCE chart (14%→21%→34%→38%→40%) is the clearest inflection signal in this list. A ₹495 Cr capex program is scaling capacity 4x by FY29. With transformers on 18–36 month backorder globally (US grid rebuild, EU renewable buildout), incremental capacity is pre-sold. At 40% ROCE, every ₹1 Cr invested returns ₹40 Lakh in annual profit.

### #6 — KSOLVES (NEW ENTRANT — Missed in v1/v2)
**127% ROCE** sustained over 5 years (143%→171%→198%→157%→127%). Not a peak — the lowest point is still 127%. Zero debt. 13 years of data. CFO/PAT = 0.89x (real cash). Pure-play AI/Salesforce engineering services → as AI adoption accelerates, demand for Salesforce AI implementation grows structurally. At ₹572 Cr MCap, this is still genuinely micro-cap with institutional discovery potential.
- **The ROCE "decline":** 198%→127% looks like a decline but it's a denominator effect — equity base is growing as the company reinvests. This is actually healthy. A 127% ROCE business that retains earnings is compounding at enormous rates.
- **Risk:** Services business = people risk. Key-person dependency. No hard asset moat. Revenue can lose a large client suddenly.

### #7 — MAYURUNIQ
BMW/Mercedes tier-1 supplier. Automotive synthetic leather qualification requires 2–3 years of testing — this is a legal/technical switching cost. ROCE just crossed 20% and is accelerating. D/E = 0.01. P/E = 14.9x is cheap for this moat quality.

### #8 — CRIZAC
The cheapest valuation (13.2x P/E) for any 50%+ ROCE company in the merged universe. Zero debt. The risk is clear: UK visa policy tightened in January 2025, and the revenue impact was visible in Q1 FY27 (−4.3% YoY). **Do not enter until Q2 FY27 results (expected October–November 2026) confirm revenue recovery.**

### #9 — SHILCTECH
Zero debt + 51% ROCE + capacity doubling. The Q1 FY27 miss (Revenue −15%, PAT −50%) is real but the cause (permit delay + shipment timing) is one-time in nature. The structural demand for renewable step-up transformers from US and EU utilities is multi-year. Maximum 25% of intended position size until Q2 FY27 confirms recovery.

### #10 — ACCELYA (NEW ENTRANT — Missed in v1/v2)
Global airlines' revenue accounting + cargo SaaS. Recurring subscription revenue (airlines cannot switch mid-year — contracts lock in 3–5 years). ROCE = 49% at 15.7x P/E with zero debt and 12 years of data. CFO/PAT = 1.04x. The ROCE declining (57%→49%) is noted — but even at 49%, it far exceeds the 20% gate. Vista Equity Partners (global SaaS PE leader) ownership provides M&A support and product road-map discipline. At ₹1,633 Cr MCap, institutional ownership is still building.
