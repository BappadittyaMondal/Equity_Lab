## 1. Executive Summary & The "Concord Control Archetype" Equation

The **Concord Control Systems Archetype** represents the most lucrative structural lifecycle in Indian equities:
$$\text{Composite Score} = \frac{\text{Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL)} \times \text{Book-to-Bill Ratio} \times \text{ROCE}}{10}$$
Where:
1. **Sovereign Capex Shield:** The company operates in a regulated, high-entry-barrier sector (RDSO Railways, AS9100D Aerospace, MoD Defence, or CEA Power Grid) with long 18–36 month customer qualification barriers.
2. **Book-to-Bill Visibility (O/R >= 1.5x to 10x):** Firm contract backlog guarantees revenue compounding over the next 24–36 months, eliminating demand volatility.
3. **Operating Leverage (DOL > 2.0):** Fixed plant and machinery overheads are fully absorbed; incremental top-line growth flows disproportionately to operating profit and EPS.
4. **Pristine Balance Sheet (ROCE >= 20%, Debt/Equity <= 0.35):** Expansion is funded through internal cash accruals rather than dilutive equity or high-interest debt.
5. **Pre-Fly Momentum Ignition:** Price trades above 50-DMA and 200-DMA with healthy momentum (RSI 55–75).
