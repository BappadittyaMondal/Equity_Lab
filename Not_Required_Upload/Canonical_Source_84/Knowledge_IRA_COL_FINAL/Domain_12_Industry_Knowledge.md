# Domain 12 — Industry Knowledge
Version: 1.0 | Status: Production Ready

---

## Purpose
Maintain a structured understanding of major industries, their key value drivers, competitive dynamics, and sector-specific metrics — enabling context-aware analysis rather than generic evaluation.

---

## Industry Reference Framework
For each industry, research should establish: (a) key demand drivers, (b) key cost drivers, (c) industry-specific metrics, (d) typical competitive structure, (e) regulatory environment, (f) cyclicality profile.

### 1. Manufacturing
- Demand drivers: Industrial capex cycle, export demand, domestic consumption.
- Key metrics: Capacity utilization, order book, EBITDA margin, asset turnover.

### 2. Capital Goods
- Demand drivers: Infrastructure investment, industrial capex, government spending.
- Key metrics: Order inflow, order book-to-sales ratio, execution timelines.

### 3. Defence
- Demand drivers: Government defence budget, indigenization policy (Make in India), export orders.
- Key metrics: Order book, contract execution timelines, R&D intensity.

### 4. Electric Equipment
- Demand drivers: Power sector capex, industrial automation, renewable energy buildout.
- Key metrics: Order inflow, raw material cost pass-through ability (copper, aluminum, steel).

### 5. Power
- Demand drivers: Electricity demand growth, industrial/commercial consumption, renewable transition.
- Key metrics: Plant load factor (PLF), tariff structure, fuel cost pass-through, regulatory asset base (for regulated utilities).

### 6. Banking
- Demand drivers: Credit growth, deposit mobilization, economic cycle.
- Key metrics: Net Interest Margin (NIM), Gross/Net NPA ratio, Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR), Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR), Credit-Deposit ratio, CASA ratio.

### 7. NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Companies)
- Demand drivers: Credit demand in underserved/niche segments, cost of borrowing.
- Key metrics: AUM growth, NIM, asset quality (GNPA/NNPA), Cost of Funds, leverage (Debt/Equity), liability mix diversity.

### 8. Insurance
- Demand drivers: Financial penetration, risk awareness, regulatory mandates.
- Key metrics: New Business Premium (NBP), Value of New Business (VNB) margin, Persistency ratio, Embedded Value, Combined Ratio (for general insurance).

### 9. Pharma
- Demand drivers: Domestic formulation demand, export markets (US generics, regulated markets), chronic disease prevalence.
- Key metrics: R&D spend as % of sales, ANDA/DMF filings, USFDA facility compliance status, molecule/product mix.

### 10. Healthcare
- Demand drivers: Healthcare infrastructure investment, insurance penetration, medical tourism.
- Key metrics: Occupancy rate, Average Revenue Per Occupied Bed (ARPOB), case mix.

### 11. IT (Information Technology)
- Demand drivers: Global enterprise IT spending, digital transformation, outsourcing trends.
- Key metrics: Revenue per employee, attrition rate, deal Total Contract Value (TCV), client concentration, constant currency growth.

### 12. Auto
- Demand drivers: Consumer income levels, financing availability, replacement cycle, EV transition.
- Key metrics: Volume growth (units sold), average selling price (ASP), market share by segment.

### 13. Auto Ancillary
- Demand drivers: OEM production volumes, aftermarket demand, export orders.
- Key metrics: Content per vehicle, client diversification, raw material cost pass-through.

### 14. Chemicals
- Demand drivers: Global specialty/commodity chemical cycles, agrochemical demand, China+1 sourcing shift.
- Key metrics: Capacity utilization, product mix (specialty vs. commodity), feedstock cost trends.

### 15. Metals
- Demand drivers: Global industrial demand, infrastructure/construction activity, China demand-supply dynamics.
- Key metrics: Realizations per tonne, capacity utilization, cost of production per tonne, EBITDA/tonne.

### 16. Mining
- Demand drivers: Global commodity demand, domestic infrastructure needs.
- Key metrics: Production volumes, reserve life, cost per tonne extracted, regulatory/environmental clearances.

### 17. Real Estate
- Demand drivers: Income growth, interest rates, urbanization, affordability.
- Key metrics: Pre-sales (bookings), collections, launch pipeline, inventory unsold, debt-to-equity, RERA compliance.

### 18. Infrastructure
- Demand drivers: Government capex, PPP/BOT project awards, private investment cycle.
- Key metrics: Order book, execution pace, working capital cycle, project-level leverage.

### 19. Telecom
- Demand drivers: Data consumption growth, subscriber additions, tariff trends.
- Key metrics: Average Revenue Per User (ARPU), subscriber market share, capex intensity (spectrum, network).

### 20. Consumer
- Demand drivers: Discretionary/staple spending, rural vs. urban demand trends, premiumization.
- Key metrics: Volume growth, gross margin, distribution reach, brand market share.

### 21. Retail
- Demand drivers: Consumer spending, footfall trends, e-commerce penetration.
- Key metrics: Same-store sales growth (SSSG), revenue per square foot, inventory turnover.

### 22. Logistics
- Demand drivers: Trade volumes, e-commerce growth, supply chain reconfiguration.
- Key metrics: Fleet utilization, freight rates, warehousing capacity utilization.

### 23. Textiles
- Demand drivers: Export demand, domestic apparel consumption, cotton/synthetic fiber price cycles.
- Key metrics: Capacity utilization, realizations, raw material cost pass-through.

### 24. Agriculture
- Demand drivers: Monsoon patterns, crop prices, government MSP policy, rural income.
- Key metrics: Acreage trends, yield per hectare, input cost inflation (fertilizer, seeds).

### 25. Renewable Energy
- Demand drivers: Government decarbonization targets, PPA (Power Purchase Agreement) tenders, cost of solar/wind generation.
- Key metrics: Capacity addition (MW), plant load factor, tariff realized, project financing cost.

## Application Framework for Research
1. Identify the 3-5 most material industry-specific metrics before evaluating any company within that sector.
2. Assess where the industry stands in its own cycle (early recovery, mid-cycle expansion, late-cycle peak, downturn).
3. Benchmark company performance against industry-specific metrics, not generic ratios alone.

---

## Red Flags / Cautions
- Applying generic ratio benchmarks across industries with fundamentally different capital and margin structures (e.g., comparing IT margins to real estate margins).
- Ignoring industry-specific regulatory or policy triggers that can materially alter sector economics.

---

## Universal Rule Applied to This Domain
Industry classifications and metrics listed here are structural reference points — always validate current cycle positioning and policy environment with live data rather than assuming static conditions.

---

## Worked Example
Two "banks" compared on generic P/E look similar, but Bank A has NIM 4.2%, GNPA 1.8%, CAR 16% vs Bank B's NIM 3.1%, GNPA 4.5%, CAR 12%. Industry-specific metrics reveal Bank A is structurally stronger — a generic P/E-only comparison would have missed this entirely.
