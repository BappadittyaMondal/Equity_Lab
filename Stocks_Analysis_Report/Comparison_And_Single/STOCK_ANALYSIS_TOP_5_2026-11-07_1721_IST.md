### Stream 3: StockBee Trading Methodology (Pradeep Bonde)
- **Episodic Pivots (EP):** When an unexpected fundamental catalyst occurs (earnings blowout, multi-hundred crore contract), the stock gaps up on 3x–5x average volume. That single session marks the beginning of a multi-week institutional mark-up.
- **Trend Discipline:** Ride the stock above the 10 EMA / 20 EMA. Never chase extended candles; buy pullbacks to the moving average anchor with predefined 0.5%–1% capital risk.

### Stream 4: 10X Multibagger Selection Matrix & Trap Detection (Himanshu Sharma)
- **The 5-Step Filter:**
  1. Scalability of Addressable Market (TAM).
  2. Operating Leverage (Revenue growing faster than expenses).
  3. Working Capital & Cash Conversion (CFO/EBITDA > 80%).
  4. Management Transparency (Order book conversion vs PR hype).
  5. Technical Base Accumulation on Weekly Charts.
- **Unmasking Traps:** Contrast companies with real cash flows (Zen Tech, Garware) against high-debt, narrative-heavy turnarounds (Sterlite Tech).

### Stream 5: Technical DNA & Price Action Architecture
- Use Volume Profile Visible Range (VPVR) to identify where institutions have parked their cost base.
- Identify liquidity sweeps below prior swing lows followed by strong daily closes above key EMAs.

### Stream 6: Structural Supercycle & Market Timing (Sushil Kedia)
- "CNX IT going into all-time high—large cap IT stocks will double before next Diwali."
- Cyclical bull markets are punctuated by sharp 3–5% shakeouts designed to flush weak retail hands. These dips are structural buying opportunities for disciplined capital.
