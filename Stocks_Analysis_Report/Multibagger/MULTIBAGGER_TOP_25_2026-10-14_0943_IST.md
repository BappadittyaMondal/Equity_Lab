## 1. Mathematical Foundation of Multifold Returns (6M to 2Y Horizon)

The total return multiplier of any equity over a 6-to-24 month horizon follows the dual-engine equation:
$$\text{Total Return Multiplier} = \left( 1 + \frac{\Delta \text{EPS}}{\text{EPS}_0} \right) \times \left( \frac{\text{Exit P/E}}{\text{Entry P/E}} \right) + \text{Dividends}$$

### The Two High-Probability Multifold Engines:
1. **Engine 1: Explosive Operational Earnings Growth (PAT Growth >= 40% to 500%+):**  
   - Guaranteed by **Book-to-Bill Ratios >= 1.5x to 11.6x** (multi-year sovereign contract backlogs eliminate demand risk).
   - High **Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL >= 2.0x)** where fixed plant costs are already absorbed, allowing gross margins to drop straight to PAT.
   - High **ROCE >= 20% to 50%**, ensuring growth is generated through high asset turns rather than equity dilution.
2. **Engine 2: Valuation Re-Rating & Multiple Expansion (P/E Headroom):**  
   - Stocks with P/E multiples between **12x and 45x** that grow earnings at 50% to 200% experience twin compounding: earnings double AND valuation multiples re-rate from 20x to 40x+, producing **3x to 5x net returns**.
   - **Crucial Mathematical Risk Guard (Multiple Compression Filter):** Stocks already trading at extreme multiples (>150x P/E, like PTCIL or Azad) are penalized because any multiple derating offsets earnings growth.
