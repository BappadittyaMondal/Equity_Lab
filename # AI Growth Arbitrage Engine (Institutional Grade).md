\# Strategy 02 – Growth vs Market Arbitrage Engine (GMAE)

\#\# Objective

The purpose of this strategy is to identify companies where the \*\*underlying business is compounding much faster than the stock price\*\*. The AI should detect situations where the market has not yet fully recognized the company's improving fundamentals, creating a potential valuation arbitrage opportunity.

Unlike traditional DCF, this strategy focuses on the \*\*gap between business performance and market performance\*\*.

\---

\# Core Investment Thesis

\> \*\*A great business does not always become an expensive stock immediately.\*\*

If the company's:

\- Sales  
\- EPS  
\- Net Profit  
\- Free Cash Flow  
\- ROCE  
\- ROE

are improving rapidly while the stock price is underperforming or stagnant, there may be a temporary market inefficiency.

The AI should identify these opportunities before the market fully prices them in.

\- Equity dilution is significant.  
\- Profit growth is not supported by operating cash flow.  
\- The stock is already trading at an extreme valuation despite strong growth.

\# Strategy 03 – Growth Already Priced In Engine (GAPIE)

\#\# Objective

The purpose of this strategy is to identify companies where the \*\*stock price has appreciated much faster than the underlying business fundamentals\*\*, suggesting that future growth expectations may already be reflected in the valuation.

Unlike the Growth Arbitrage Engine, this strategy searches for \*\*Expectation Risk\*\* rather than Opportunity.

The AI should answer one question:

\> \*\*Has the market already priced in too much future growth?\*\*

\---

\# Core Investment Thesis

A company can be an excellent business but still be a poor investment if investors have already priced in years of future growth.

\# Important AI Principle

The objective is \*\*not to eliminate all risk\*\*.

The objective is to avoid \*\*permanent loss of capital\*\* while maximizing long-term compounding.

The AI should distinguish between:

\- \*\*Price Volatility\*\* (temporary and often creates opportunity)  
\- \*\*Business Risk\*\* (can permanently destroy intrinsic value)

Only businesses with strong financials, durable competitive advantages, disciplined management, and resilient business models should receive the highest Risk Intelligence Score.
