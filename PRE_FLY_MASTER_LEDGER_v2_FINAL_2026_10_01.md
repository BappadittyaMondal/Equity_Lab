$$\text{Score} = (\text{ROCE}_\text{TTM} \times 0.30) + (\text{PAT YoY}\% \times 0.20) + \left(\frac{100 - P/E}{10}\right) \times 0.15 + (\text{CFO/PAT} \times 10 \times 0.25) + (\text{BS\_Purity} \times 0.10)$$
