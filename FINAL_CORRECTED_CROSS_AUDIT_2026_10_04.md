## WHY 4 STOCKS WERE REMOVED (Evidence-Based, Not Ad-Hoc)

### 1. PIXTRANS (Was #1 → Eliminated)
**Thesis:** "Fastest earnings acceleration, PAT +89%"  
**Evidence that broke it:**
- BSE/NSE regulatory filing (Ind AS 109 note): Q1 FY27 Other Income included a **Fair Value Through Profit or Loss (FVTPL) adjustment on investments of ₹25.29 Crore.**
- Standalone Q1 FY27 PAT = ₹48.29 Cr. Of this, **₹25.29 Cr (52.4%) was an unrealized mark-to-market investment gain** — not operational earnings from rubber belt manufacturing.
- In Q4 FY26, the same FVTPL line was a **LOSS of ₹22.52 Crore** — showing extreme non-operational MTM volatility.
- Revenue grew +22% YoY (not +10% as Report A claimed), but operational earnings growth was modest.
- FY26 full-year PAT fell **-5.3%** (₹107 Cr vs ₹113 Cr). Standalone FY26 PAT fell **-12.6%**.
- Q4 FY26 PAT fell **-10.7%** YoY with EBITDA margin compressing from 20.1% to 18.1%.
- **Methodology gate violated:** The "+89% PAT" headline was an accounting artifact from FVTPL MTM swings, not sustainable operating earnings acceleration. The core rubber belt business grew earnings modestly while full-year profits declined.

### 2. SHILCTECH (Was #9 → Eliminated)
