## 3. Microscopic Forensic Accounting Audit: De-Coding Ind AS 116

> [!NOTE]
> ### FORENSIC DE-MYSTIFICATION: THE LEASE ACCOUNTING ILLUSION
> When inexperienced screeners look at Awfis's balance sheet, they see:
> *Total Borrowings: ₹1,525 Crore vs Reserves: ₹537 Crore*.
> **This is an accounting artifact of Ind AS 116, NOT real financial debt.**
> 
> Under Ind AS 116 rules:
> 1. Future lease payment commitments to landlords are discounted to present value and booked as a **Lease Liability** (₹1,525 Cr under Borrowings).
> 2. Simultaneously, an equal **Right-of-Use (ROU) Asset** is created under Fixed Assets (₹1,756 Cr).
> 3. Rent expense is stripped out of operating costs and replaced with **Depreciation on ROU assets** (₹119 Cr in Q2) and **Finance Costs on lease liabilities** (₹49 Cr in Q2).
> 
> **The Real Operational Metric:** Watch **Cash EBITDA** and **Free Cash Flow**. Awfis generated **₹616 Crore CFO** and **₹406 Crore FCF** in FY26. It operates with a **Net Debt of -0.12x (Net Cash Positive)**.
