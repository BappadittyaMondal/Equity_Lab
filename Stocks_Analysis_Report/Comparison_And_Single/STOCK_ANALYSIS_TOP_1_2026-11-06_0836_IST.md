## 4. Macro, Geopolitical & Government Policy DNA

1. **Indian Railways Mega Capex (₹2.52 Lakh Crore Outlay):** The Indian government continues unprecedented capital expenditure into rail modernization under the National Rail Plan (NRP) 2030, targeting freight modal share expansion from 27% to 45% (Mission 3000 MT freight throughput).
2. **Dedicated Freight Corridors (EDFC & WDFC):** Full commercial commissioning of freight corridors necessitates specialized 25-tonne axle-load wagons (BOXNHL, BOXNS, BCFCM cement rakes, and container flats). Texmaco is India's leading private wagon manufacturer alongside Titagarh Rail Systems.
3. **Executable Backlog Moat (₹9,923 Crore):** The company commands a fortress order backlog of ~₹9,923 Cr, providing multi-year cash flow visibility. Wagon delivery ramp-ups in H2 FY27 will reverse Q2 operational weakness.
4. **Strategic De-risking via JV Monetization:** By diluting 16% in Texmaco Touax to 34%, Texmaco extracted ₹76 Cr in cash while maintaining strategic alignment, reducing capital lock-up in railcar leasing assets.
5. **Geopolitical & Export Reach:** Texmaco exports specialized railway wagons and steel bridge structures to Africa, the Middle East, and Australia, providing high-margin counter-cyclical buffers against domestic tender pauses.
