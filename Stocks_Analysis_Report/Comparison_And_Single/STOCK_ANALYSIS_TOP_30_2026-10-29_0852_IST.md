### [FLASH 1] Garden Reach Shipbuilders (GRSE) Inks Historic Naval Pipeline
**Kolkata / New Delhi:** Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd (GRSE) has solidified its status as the prime contractor for the Indian Navy’s marquee **₹33,000 Crore Next-Generation Corvette (NGC)** program. Having emerged as the lowest bidder (L1), price negotiations have successfully concluded, positioning the contract for final cabinet security committee sign-off.
- **Capacity Preparedness:** GRSE has proactively initiated a **₹2,896 Crore greenfield shipyard facility at Raichak, West Bengal**, expanding its simultaneous shipbuilding berths from 28 to 43 platforms.
- **Execution Run-Rate:** Despite a temporary reduction in its reported backlog to ₹13,596 Crore due to rapid vessel deliveries, management highlighted that upcoming RFPs—including the mega **P-17 Bravo frigates (~₹70,000 Crore)**—ensure an unprecedented 10-year sovereign revenue runway.
- **Stock Impact:** *Strong Accumulate.* The formal NGC contract award will act as a major catalyst, unlocking multi-year institutional upgrades.

### [FLASH 2] Bondada Engineering Pierces ₹10,000 Cr Milestone with Grid BESS Contract
**Hyderabad:** Infrastructure and renewable EPC specialist Bondada Engineering reported a massive jump in its confirmed order backlog, crossing **₹10,023 Crore**—a multifold increase from ₹7,147 Crore at the end of FY26.
- **Technological Breakthrough:** The company received a major Letter of Intent from Gujarat Industries Power Company (GIPCL) worth **₹239.25 Crore** to establish India's largest grid-scale **Vanadium Redox Flow Battery Energy Storage System (VRFB BESS)** (20 MW / 120 MWh) in Vadodara, featuring a comprehensive 10-year O&M contract.
- **Institutional Milestone:** BSE and NSE have formally approved Bondada’s migration from the SME exchange to the **Main Board**, removing liquidity barriers and enabling large institutional mutual funds to initiate coverage.
- **Stock Impact:** *High Institutional Momentum.* Multi-year revenue visibility and battery tech diversification justify strong multiple re-rating.

### [FLASH 3] BLS International Surpasses 100-Country Mark, Deploys ₹2,500 Cr Capex
**New Delhi / Dubai:** In a landmark announcement, visa outsourcing and digital sovereign service provider BLS International confirmed its operational network has breached the **100-country milestone**, managing consular mandates across 46 global governments.
- **Key Contract Wins:** The expansion is underpinned by a 5-year global visa contract from the Slovak Republic (spanning 80+ nations), extended mandates from Cyprus, and MEA India's consular operations in China.
- **M&A Execution:** BLS finalized the 100% all-cash buyout of AI banking tech firm *Atyati Technologies* (₹157 Crore) and fully consolidated its Japanese arm.
- **Management Guidance:** Leadership outlined a aggressive **₹2,500 Crore 3-4 year capex plan** targeted at doubling group turnover by 2030 with zero debt.
- **Stock Impact:** *High Conviction Value Compounder.* At 12.8x P/E with >₹3,200 Cr cash surplus, BLS represents one of the strongest risk-reward profiles on Dalal Street.

### [FLASH 4] Action Construction Equipment (ACE) Operationalizes Kato JV
**Faridabad / Palwal:** Action Construction Equipment Ltd has completed the formal operational rollout of its 50:50 joint venture, **ACE KATO Pvt. Ltd.**, with Japan’s Kato Works.
- **Manufacturing Footprint:** The joint venture is investing approximately **₹200 Crore** across an 11-acre dedicated facility in Palwal, Haryana, targeting high-capacity truck cranes, crawler cranes, and rough-terrain cranes.
- **Financial Projections:** Management guides for incremental revenues of **₹300 to ₹400 Crore** within 2–3 years as local manufacturing replaces Chinese imports and exports commence through Kato’s international distribution channels.
- **Stock Impact:** *Bullish Trajectory.* Post-CEV-5 transition normalization and heavy crane localisation provide strong operating leverage.
