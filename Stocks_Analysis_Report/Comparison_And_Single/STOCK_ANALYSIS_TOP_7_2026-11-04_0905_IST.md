## 1. Ashish Kacholia (Lucky Investment Managers) — "Private Equity in Public Markets"

### Stock 1: E2E Networks Limited (NSE: E2E)
* **Transaction & Disclosed Entry:** 
  - **Stake Acquired:** 1,77,043 shares (~1.10% equity)
  - **Date of Disclosure:** Disclosed in Q2 FY25 shareholding filing (quarter ending 30-Sep-2024; exchange filing mid-October 2024).
  - **Entry Price Band:** Traded between ₹1,800 – ₹2,600 during Q2 FY25 accumulation window.
  - **Subsequent Event / CMP:** Acquired an additional institutional anchor when Larsen & Toubro (L&T) announced acquisition of a 21% stake for ₹1,407 Cr on 05-Nov-2024 at ₹3,622.25/share (preferential) and ₹2,750/share (secondary). Current Market Price (CMP) post-corporate action/split: ~₹494.65 (Market Cap: ~₹10,112 Cr).
* **Why He Entered (Core Thesis):**
  - E2E Networks is India's leading sovereign cloud and AI GPU infrastructure provider (Nvidia Cloud Partner). It rents out high-end computing clusters (H100, A100 GPUs) to AI startups, enterprises, and research institutions at ~40–50% discount to AWS/Azure.
* **Mathematical & Operational Logic:**
  - **Operating Leverage Convexity ($\Omega > 2.5$):** Once high-end GPU clusters are procured and racked, incremental client additions generate near 80% gross margins.
  - **Asset Turnover Inflection:** Massive capacity utilization surge driven by explosive domestic demand for generative AI training and inference.
* **Psychology & Behavioral Edge:**
  - **Thematic Front-Running:** Kacholia identified the acute structural shortage of localized computing power in India before mainstream Tier-1 institutional funds could participate due to market-cap constraints.
* **Catalyst / Triggering Event:**
  - Acceleration of IndiaAI Mission government grants and strategic corporate interest (subsequently validated by L&T's ₹1,407 Cr strategic buyout).

* **Why He Entered (Core Thesis):**
  - Precision Camshafts holds ~70% market share in passenger vehicle camshafts in India and supplies global OEMs (Ford, GM, Toyota). Traditional ICE cash cows fund its strategic electric mobility pivot through its Dutch subsidiary, **EMOSS Mobile Systems**.
* **Mathematical & Operational Logic:**
  - **Commercial EV Retrofitment Moat:** EMOSS converts existing heavy diesel trucks and buses to pure electric drivetrains at a fraction of the cost of buying new electric commercial vehicles.
* **Psychology & Behavioral Edge:**
  - **SMILE Principles (Small in size, Medium in experience, Large in aspiration, Extra-large in market potential):** Kedia identified an established, dividend-paying auto component maker trading at modest multiples that possessed a hidden, unvalued call option on European commercial EV fleet electrification.
* **Catalyst / Triggering Event:**
  - Scaling up of domestic EV component manufacturing lines in Solapur utilizing EMOSS technology.
